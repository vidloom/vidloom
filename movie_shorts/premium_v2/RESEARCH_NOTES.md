# Research-backed editorial target (October 2026)

The useful public evidence does not support a universal "viral effect pack."
YouTube says Shorts ranking responds to chose-to-view, average view duration,
average percentage viewed, and satisfaction signals. A study of 1,178 English
million-view Shorts found a median hook length of 11 words, but explicitly does
not establish causation. The practical interpretation for this reference edit:

1. State the concrete conflict immediately.
2. Withhold the resolution, not the subject.
3. Make every cut add information, scale, emotion, or consequence.
4. Design for comprehension with sound off, then make sound reward headphones.
5. End immediately after the payoff; do not add an outro or generic CTA.
