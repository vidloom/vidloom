Privacy policy

Vidloom Automation is a private, single-owner production application. This policy explains its limited use of Google user data.

Data accessed

With the owner’s consent, Vidloom uses YouTube authorization credentials and the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status, and scheduled publication time, and read the resulting video identifier and status.

Use and storage

Google OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are used only by the private production workflow. The browser control center never receives these credentials. Operational records may store a YouTube video ID, URL, upload status, and schedule.

Sharing and sale

Vidloom does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties. Processing is limited to GitHub Actions, the owner’s production machine, Google/YouTube, and Cloudflare for the private control interface.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access in Google Account permissions at any time, delete the OAuth secrets from GitHub, and remove operational records from the repository. Revocation prevents future API access.

Contact

Questions are handled by the Vidloom account owner through the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
